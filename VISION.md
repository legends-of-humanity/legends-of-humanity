# ERD: Legends of Humanity

## Dominant Positivity 💛

ERD celebrates greatness. Not power. Not wealth. Not domination.

Every card in ERD is someone or something that made the world better.
People who dropped their ego for humanity. For animals. For nature. For life.

## Who belongs in ERD?

✅ YES:
- Peace makers who chose nonviolence when violence was easier
- Nature guardians who protected forests, oceans, and animals
- Truth tellers who risked everything to expose injustice
- Knowledge liberators who made education and information free
- Healers who dedicated their lives to saving others
- Community builders who brought people together
- Artists who inspired humanity to be better
- Scientists who solved problems for everyone, not just the rich
- Activists who fought for the voiceless — humans, animals, nature
- Anyone who dropped their ego for something bigger than themselves

❌ NO:
- Dictators, warmongers, arms dealers
- People who hoard wealth while others starve
- Manipulators, propagandists, exploiters
- Corporations that destroy for profit
- Anyone whose "greatness" came at the cost of others' suffering

## The Game

You don't win by dominating. You win by building.

Victory conditions:
- 🕊️ Harmony — Peace, trust, dignity, and low conflict
- 💡 Innovation — Knowledge, technology, and discovery for all
- 🤝 Cooperation — Coordination, community, and mutual aid
- 💚 Love — Care for life: humans, animals, nature, the planet

There is no "Force Victory". Domination is not celebrated here.

## The Mission

ERD is a living monument to the best of humanity.

It teaches that the strongest people in history were not the ones
who took the most, but the ones who gave the most.

Every card is a reminder that greatness means service.

Dominant Positivity. 💛
