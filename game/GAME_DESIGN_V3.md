# ERD Game Design v3 — Dominant Positivity

## Philosophy
You don't win by destroying. You win by building.
No Force Victory. No Domination. Only creation.

## 4 Victory Conditions

| Victory | Condition | What it means |
|---------|-----------|---------------|
| 🕊️ Harmony | Harmony ≥ 8 + Trust ≥ 5 | Peace, dignity, and trust flourish |
| 💡 Innovation | 3+ Knowledge cards + Discovery ≥ 7 | Science and creativity light the way |
| 🤝 Cooperation | Community ≥ 8 + all players positive | Everyone is lifted, no one left behind |
| 💚 Love | Nature ≥ 5 + Harmony ≥ 5 + Community ≥ 5 | Balance of all that matters — life thrives |

## 4 Tracked Values (all positive)
- 🕊️ Harmony — Peace, trust, reconciliation
- 💡 Discovery — Knowledge, innovation, creativity
- 🤝 Community — Cooperation, solidarity, mutual aid
- 🌱 Nature — Ecology, animals, earth healing

## Shared Value
- 🌍 World Health — starts at 5, goes up or down based on actions

## 7 Resources
- 💛 Love — the universal resource, generated by all cards
- 📚 Knowledge — from scientists, educators, liberators
- 🌱 Seeds — from nature guardians, reforestation
- 🎵 Culture — from artists, musicians, community builders
- 🤝 Solidarity — from movements, cooperation
- ⏳ Time — spent wisely, not rushed
- ✨ Inspiration — rare, powerful, from legendary acts

## Actions (all constructive)
| Action | Cost | Effect |
|--------|------|--------|
| Heal | 💛1 🌱1 | +2 Nature, +1 World Health |
| Teach | 📚2 | +2 Discovery, +1 Community |
| Unite | 🤝2 💛1 | +2 Community, +1 Harmony |
| Create | 🎵1 ✨1 | +2 Discovery, +1 Culture bonus |
| Protect | 💛1 🤝1 | +2 Nature, +1 Harmony |
| Inspire | ✨2 | +1 to ALL values |
| Reconcile | 💛2 ⏳1 | +3 Harmony |
| Liberate | 📚1 🤝1 | +2 Community, +1 Discovery |
| Plant | 🌱2 ⏳1 | +3 Nature |
| Amplify | 🎵2 🤝1 | +2 Community, spreads joy |
