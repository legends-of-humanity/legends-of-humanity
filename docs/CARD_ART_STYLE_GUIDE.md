# ERD Card Art Style Guide

## Philosophy

Every card tells a story. The art should reflect the CULTURE, TRADITION, and ESSENCE of the entity — not generic stock imagery.

## General Principles

1. **Cultural authenticity** — Research the entity's cultural context
2. **Symbolic depth** — Use meaningful symbols, not decoration
3. **Premium feel** — This is a collectible, make it feel precious
4. **No AI slop** — Art should have intention and soul
5. **Accessible** — Beautiful to people who don't know the entity, meaningful to those who do

## Style by Entity Type

### Persons
- Portrait-style but NOT photographic
- Stylized, almost mythic quality
- Background reflects their world/era/culture
- Symbolic elements from their legacy
- Examples:
  - **Gandhi**: Spinning wheel, salt march imagery, saffron/white/green palette, Indian textile patterns in frame
  - **Mandela**: Robben Island silhouette, African geometric borders, Ubuntu color warmth
  - **Turing**: Circuit-board patterns morphing into Enigma machine, mathematical notation as texture
  - **Marie Curie**: Radium glow, laboratory glass, periodic table elements as border
  - **Malala**: Book/pen imagery, Swat Valley mountains, education symbols

### Ideas
- Abstract but grounded
- Visual metaphor for the concept
- Should make you FEEL the idea
- Examples:
  - **Nonviolence**: Hands open, no fists, flowing water breaking stone
  - **Open Source**: Interconnected nodes, transparent layers, community hands
  - **Surveillance Capitalism**: Eye made of data points, screen reflection, invisible chains

### Movements
- Collective energy, many-to-one
- Historical imagery reimagined
- Power of numbers
- Examples:
  - **Civil Rights Movement**: March imagery, linked arms, bridge symbolism
  - **Climate Activism**: Rising tide, green resistance, youth energy

### Technology
- Clean, precise, forward-looking
- Show the IMPACT not just the object
- Examples:
  - **AI**: Neural network as galaxy, both beautiful and unsettling
  - **Nuclear Energy**: Atom as sun, dual nature (power/danger)
  - **The Internet**: Global web as mycelium, connecting everything

### Resources
- Raw, powerful, earth-connected
- Show both value and cost
- Examples:
  - **Oil**: Black gold flowing, beautiful but ominous
  - **Lithium**: White metal in desert landscape, clean energy vs extraction
  - **Forest**: Deep green layers, biodiversity, ancient presence

### Countries
- NOT flags — capture the essence
- Landscape + culture + power
- Examples:
  - **China**: Great Wall perspective, scale and ambition
  - **UAE**: Desert meets glass towers, transformation

## Rarity Visual Treatment

| Rarity | Frame | Special Effect |
|--------|-------|---------------|
| Signal | Clean matte border | None |
| Archive | Subtle metallic edge | Soft inner glow |
| Relic | Etched shimmer frame | Partial foil accent |
| Legend | Luminous gold border | Multi-layer depth |
| Mythic | Prismatic constellation | Full holographic feel |

## Battle Effects (Future)

When cards interact in combat:
- **Build**: Construction sparks, building rising
- **Mobilize**: Army movement, dust clouds
- **Reveal**: Light beam cutting through darkness
- **Pressure**: Weight/gravity visual, cracks forming
- **Stabilize**: Calm wave, healing light
- **Extract**: Oil/resource flowing, earth opening
- **Disrupt**: Lightning, screen glitch, fracture
- **Reconcile**: Two lights merging, bridge forming

## Technical Specs

- **Card size**: 750 x 1050 px (standard card ratio)
- **Format**: PNG with transparency for frame overlay
- **Portrait zone**: 600 x 500 px centered
- **Safe zone**: 50px from all edges
- **File naming**: `{entity-id}-{artist-handle}.png`

## Submission

1. Pick an entity from the atlas
2. Create art following this guide
3. Submit via GitHub PR to `art/{entity-id}/`
4. Include: final PNG + process notes
5. Community votes on submissions
