3

Each Turn

The game lasts 6 turns. Each turn has 2 phases:

1. Deploy — Play 0-2 cards from your hand onto the board. Each costs resources.

2. Action — Each deployed card can perform one action: Build, Reveal, Pressure, Reconcile, and more.

Actions change 3 values: Power, Legitimacy, Harmony. Plus shared Instability.

4

4 Ways to Win

⚔️ Force Victory

Power ≥ 10. Raw domination. Fast but costly.

⚖️ Influence Victory

Legitimacy ≥ 10 + Power ≥ 5. Soft power wins.

💡 Innovation Victory

3+ Tech cards + Power ≥ 7. Technology prevails.

🕊️ Harmony Victory

Harmony ≥ 8 + Low Instability + Legitimacy ≥ 5. Hardest but strongest.
