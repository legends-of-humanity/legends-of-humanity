# ERD: Legends of Humanity 💛

**Dominant Positivity.**

A card game celebrating the greatest humans who ever lived — people who dropped their ego for humanity, for animals, for nature, for life.

## 🌐 Play Now

**[legends-of-humanity.onrender.com](https://legends-of-humanity.onrender.com)**

## What is ERD?

Every card is a real person, idea, or movement that made the world better.

- 🕊️ **Peace Makers** — Gandhi, MLK, Mandela, Desmond Tutu
- 🌱 **Nature Guardians** — Jane Goodall, Wangari Maathai, Greta Thunberg
- 📚 **Knowledge Liberators** — Tim Berners-Lee, Aaron Swartz, Malala
- 💛 **Healers** — Marie Curie, and those who gave their lives for others
- 🎵 **Community Builders** — Bob Marley, and those who united people through love
- ✊ **Dignity Fighters** — Rosa Parks, and those who stood up by sitting down

## How to Win

You don't win by dominating. You win by building.

| Victory | How |
|---------|-----|
| 🕊️ Harmony | Peace, trust, and dignity flourish |
| 💡 Innovation | Knowledge and creativity light the way |
| 🤝 Cooperation | Everyone is lifted, no one left behind |
| 💚 Love | Nature, harmony, and community in balance |

**There is no Force Victory. Domination is not celebrated here.**

## Who Belongs in ERD?

✅ People who dropped their ego for something bigger
✅ Movements that chose love over hate
✅ Ideas that freed knowledge and dignity
✅ Anyone who served life — human, animal, nature

❌ No dictators. No warmongers. No exploiters.

## Quick Start

```bash
git clone https://github.com/legends-of-humanity/legends-of-humanity.git
cd legends-of-humanity/site
npm install
node server.js
# Open http://localhost:3000
```
